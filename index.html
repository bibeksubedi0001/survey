How to Use

  1. Open the menu (☰) to switch between sections. Allow Location, Camera, and Microphone permissions when prompted.
  2. NEW SURVEY — Tap CAPTURE LOCATION; the app refines GPS until accuracy reaches ≤ 2 m. Tap START CAMERA, then CAPTURE PHOTO. Fill the form, then SAVE SURVEY.
  3. RECORDS — view, edit, delete, or EXPORT EXCEL. Data is stored locally on this device (offline-safe).
  4. FIELD REPORTS (Chief Officer, Leakage, Pressure, Area Survey) — each works the same way: fill the form, capture stamped photos, dictate remarks via MIC, and export to Excel.
  5. NRW REPORT — Upload customer reading + SCADA workbooks to auto-build a Baishakh-style report.

Tip: For best GPS accuracy, stand outdoors with clear sky for 10–15 seconds before capturing location.
