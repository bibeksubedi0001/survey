How to Use

  1. Open this page on your mobile or laptop. Allow Location and Camera permissions when prompted.
  2. Tap CAPTURE LOCATION — the app continuously refines GPS until accuracy reaches ≤ 2 m (target). You can tap STOP & USE BEST any time to accept the best reading so far.
  3. Tap START CAMERA, then CAPTURE PHOTO for each angle. Use SWITCH to toggle front/rear. You may also upload existing pictures.
  4. Fill in customer + connection details and tap SAVE SURVEY.
  5. Open the RECORDS tab to view, edit, delete, or EXPORT EXCEL.
  6. Data is stored locally on this device (offline-safe). Always export Excel before clearing.

Tip: For best GPS accuracy, stand outdoors with clear sky for 10–15 seconds before capturing location.
